# TALH Figure Plan (AAAI-27)

This document defines the argumentative role, placement, layout, and data source of each paper figure. It follows the AAAI two-column format: 3.3 in for a single column and 7.0 in across both columns. Data-driven figures are exported as vector PDFs with 300 DPI PNG previews; video-frame composites are exported as 300 DPI or higher PNG/PDF files.

## 1. Visual Argument

The three contributions require complementary visual evidence:

1. **System contribution:** TALH moves most denoising evaluations to a low-resolution latent space and exposes two quality--efficiency operating points, TALH-Q and TALH-E.
2. **Method contribution:** Dynamic resolution handoff is decomposed into TAA trajectory alignment, CLL cross-resolution lifting, and HTR high-resolution trajectory re-entry.
3. **Training contribution:** TAA and CLL share model-internal supervision from the frozen generator, without external paired videos, external super-resolution weights, or an additional teacher.

The main-paper figures follow the sequence **real-frame result and acceleration mechanism -> method overview -> system benefit -> component mechanism**. The extended qualitative panel is retained as supplementary evidence. We plan four main-paper figures and five supplementary figures.

## 4. Figure 2: TALH Framework and Model-Internal Training

### 4.1 Placement

- **Source location:** After the introductory paragraph that decomposes TALH into TAA, CLL, and HTR, either immediately before or after the contribution list.
- **Cross-references:** Cite Fig. 2 in Sections 3.1, 3.2, and 3.6 for its inference, training, and re-entry portions.
- **LaTeX form:** Top-aligned `figure*`.

### 4.2 Argumentative Role

The figure should answer three questions at once:

1. Why TALH accelerates sampling: a low-resolution prefix replaces most high-resolution denoising evaluations.
2. Why TALH uses three modules: trajectory gap, cross-scale lifting error, and high-resolution residual refinement are handled separately.
3. How TALH is trained: both training-pair families are generated by the same frozen Wan model.

### 4.3 Recommended Layout

Use two horizontal bands, read from left to right.

#### Upper band: inference and operating points

1. **Sampling timelines:**
   - Native-HR: 50 high-resolution evaluations, all shown as warm-red or dark-gray blocks.
   - TALH-Q: 40 low-resolution evaluations followed by 10 high-resolution evaluations, with a handoff marker at step 40.
   - TALH-E: 45 low-resolution evaluations followed by 5 high-resolution evaluations, with a handoff marker at step 45.
   - Above the timelines, annotate the functional progression as `Structure & Motion -> Texture & Detail`.

2. **Handoff pipeline:**
   - `LR Prefix` -> `TAA at step s` -> `Aligned Clean LR Latent` -> `CLL` -> `Lifted Clean HR Latent` -> `HTR` -> `HR Suffix`.
   - Inside TAA: `LoRA; base model frozen`.
   - Inside CLL: `Clean LR -> Clean HR`.
   - Inside HTR: `Target-resolution re-noise`.
   - Mark `E_traj(s)`, `E_lift(s)`, and `E_refine(s)` at their corresponding locations. Use thin dashed error indicators rather than large warning colors.

#### Lower band: model-internal supervision

1. **Trajectory-alignment pairs:**
   - A frozen low-resolution Wan rollout produces the cached state `x_s^L` and the full-trajectory endpoint `z_T^L`.
   - The two form a TAA training pair.
   - Next to `x_s^L`, write `cached prefix = inference state`, with a compact condition label: `same prompt / seed / scheduler / CFG`.

2. **Cross-resolution lifting pairs:**
   - Frozen Wan generates an HR video.
   - One branch is encoded directly by the Wan VAE to obtain `z_0^H`; the other is first spatially downsampled in RGB and then encoded by the same Wan VAE to obtain `z_0^L`.
   - The two form a CLL training pair.

3. **Shared boundary:**
   - Enclose both flows in a region labeled `Model-Internal Supervision`.
   - At the bottom of this region, state `No external paired videos, SR weights, or extra teacher`.

### 4.4 Visual Specification

- **Workflow:** Academic Plotting, architecture-diagram workflow. If image generation is unavailable or exact label control is insufficient, use a deterministic vector implementation with the same layout.
- **Style:** Classic Accent Bar. The formal, flat style suits AAAI and remains legible in grayscale.
- **Semantic colors:**
  - Low-resolution computation: blue `#4A90D9`.
  - TAA: magenta `#CC79A7`.
  - CLL: green `#009E73`.
  - HTR and high-resolution suffix: vermillion `#D55E00`.
  - Frozen Wan, VAE, and baselines: gray `#7B8794`.
- **Avoid:** network-layer miniatures, video frames, shadows, gradients, and decorative icons. Fig. 2 explains system relationships rather than implementation internals.

### 4.5 Draft Caption

> **TALH inference and model-internal training.** TALH performs most denoising evaluations in a low-resolution latent space and applies TAA trajectory alignment, CLL cross-resolution lifting, and HTR high-resolution trajectory re-entry at the handoff. TALH-Q and TALH-E retain 10 and 5 high-resolution evaluations, respectively. Supervision for TAA and CLL is generated by the same frozen Wan model through trajectory-alignment pairs and cross-resolution lifting pairs.

## 7. Supplementary Figure S1: Extended Qualitative Comparison

### 7.1 Placement

- **Source location:** Supplementary qualitative-results section; the main text refers to it from Sec. 5.8.
- **LaTeX form:** Full-width `figure*`.
- **Goal:** Translate the tabular findings--CLL improves clarity, TAA exposes a detail--temporal trade-off, and TALH-Q/E allocate different HR-suffix budgets--into direct visual evidence.

### 7.2 Recommended Content

Use one full-width, two-panel composite of real video frames. The content-aligned reference is labeled `Native-HR (estimated)` and is distinct from the actual 720p `Native-HR Sampling` baseline in Table 1.

#### (a) Spatial detail and module contribution

- Use a detail-rich TALH-Q candidate with handoff at step 40.
- Rows: Native-HR (estimated), Trilinear@40, CLL-only@40, and TALH-Q.
- Show the same full frame for every row. Use two 2x or 3x crops at identical coordinates to compare the three 720p outputs. The estimated row provides content and structural context but is excluded from sharpness ranking.
- The panel jointly exposes the system reference, fixed interpolation, the main CLL effect, and full TALH rather than comparing final systems alone.

#### (b) Consecutive frames and temporal behavior

- Use a motion-rich TALH-E candidate with handoff at step 45.
- Rows: Native-HR (estimated), Trilinear@45, CLL-only@45, and TALH-E.
- For each row, show one full context frame followed by five crops from a short sequence, for example `t-4, t-2, t, t+2, t+4`.
- Use the panel to inspect subject shape, texture attachment, and local flicker, faithfully exposing the detail--temporal trade-off of TAA.

Prioritize crop regions containing:

- Faces, animal heads, or stable identity regions.
- Fine materials, edges, text, or small objects.
- Areas prone to flicker or deformation during motion.

The two panels may use different prompts, but every method within a panel must use the same prompt, seed, frame indices, and crop coordinates. The three handoff methods must also share the same LR prefix and sampling configuration. Do not plot pixel-error heatmaps relative to Native-HR (estimated), and do not treat it as a 720p sharpness target.

`Native-HR (estimated)` is the 640x368 video obtained by completing all 50 evaluations on the same low-resolution trajectory; it is proportionally enlarged only for layout consistency. This proxy is necessary because native 368p and native 720p sampling produce different content even under the same prompt and seed, so an actual 720p Native-HR video cannot provide a frame-aligned qualitative reference. Actual 720p Native-HR remains the baseline for every quantitative quality--efficiency comparison.

### 7.3 Sample-Selection Protocol

- Restrict candidates to the ten matched prompts in the main evaluation; do not select from unreported samples.
- Before inspecting method differences, select one detail-rich prompt and one motion-rich prompt by semantic criteria, or use a median sample under the quantitative Native-HR metrics.
- Do not show only the best TALH sample; include failure cases in the supplement.
- Use identical prompts, seeds, frame indices, and crop coordinates across methods.
- Extract lossless PNG frames from original videos to avoid hiding high-frequency differences with JPEG compression.

### 7.4 Visual Specification

- Place method names to the left of each row and frame indices above each column.
- Use thin crop boxes and matching connector lines. Avoid thick arrows that occlude content.
- Leave 1--2 mm of white space between panels. Do not use shadows or rounded-card effects.
- Keep Native-HR (estimated) neutral and preserve `(estimated)` in the row label. Color TALH-Q blue and TALH-E orange, consistent with Fig. 3.
- Add the small gray in-figure note `368p aligned reference` beside the estimated row; do not rely on the caption alone.

### 7.5 Draft Caption

> **Real video frames under matched prompts and seeds.** (a) Spatial-detail comparison at the step-40 handoff; full frames and crops contrast trilinear interpolation, CLL, and TALH-Q. (b) Consecutive local frames at the step-45 handoff reveal the detail and temporal behavior of CLL-only and TALH-E. `Native-HR (estimated)` is the content-aligned 368p full trajectory, enlarged only as a structural and motion reference; quantitative Native-HR Sampling uses actual 720p generation.
